# StatisticsForCytometry

Hello! You have discovered one of my projects-in-development. My main motivation is that I have wanted to branch out and integrate more statistical modelling into my work, but previous attempts have vanished into miscellaneous project folders after a week and never seen the light of day afterwards. Building off lessons from [Cytometry in R](https://umgcccfcsr.github.io/CytometryInR/), my hope is that with time, this series of walk-throughs can evolve into something more holistic that moves analysis beyond count, frequency, and p-value.

My goals are that over the next year, I am able to work through the following statistic textbooks, and apply them to spectral flow cytometry datasets as solved examples. 

- [Statistical Rethinking, by Richard McElreath](https://xcelab.net/rm/)

- [Modern Statistics for Modern Biology, by Susan Holmes and Wolfgang Huber](https://www.huber.embl.de/msmb/)

- Various Andrew Gelman textbooks
